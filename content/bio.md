I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I am fortunate to work under the supervision of [Tianshu Yu](https://mypage.cuhk.edu.cn/academics/yutianshu/).

My research focuses on **learning-guided optimization and search**, with an emphasis on neural solvers for routing problems, reinforcement learning, and Monte Carlo tree search. I also study how language models can support algorithm and kernel optimization through structured search and feedback.

Before my Ph.D., I conducted my master's research under the supervision of [Yan Jin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=joz7edsAAAAJ). I also worked as a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia, where I was mentored by [Lei Song](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pXDSOocAAAAJ) on scalable neural solvers for the traveling salesman problem.
